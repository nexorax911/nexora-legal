# Privacy Policy — NEXORA

Last updated: October 2, 2026

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how the NEXORA Discord bot ("the Bot", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use it in a Discord server. By using the Bot, you agree to this Policy.

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Discord identifiers:** user IDs, usernames, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, and role IDs.
- **Message data:** message content is processed to provide moderation, logging, and auto-moderation features. Logs may temporarily store message content.
- **Server activity:** join/leave events, invite usage, voice-state changes, roles, and punishments.
- **Command data:** inputs you provide to commands (for example ticket details, giveaway entries, and partnership applications).

We do **not** collect passwords, payment information, or private messages.

## 3. How We Use Information
We use the collected information only to operate and provide the Bot's features, including welcome/goodbye messages, auto-moderation, logging, tickets, giveaways, partnerships, and boost announcements. We do **not** sell your data.

## 4. Data Storage and Security
Data is stored in a private database controlled by the Bot operator. We apply reasonable technical measures to protect it. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 5. Data Sharing
We do not share your data with third parties, except where required by law or to comply with Discord's Terms of Service and Developer Policies.

## 6. Data Retention
Data is kept only as long as necessary to provide the Bot's features. Server administrators may request deletion of their server's data at any time.

## 7. Your Rights
You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting us through the support server below.

## 8. Children
The Bot is not intended for users under the minimum age required by Discord's Terms of Service.

## 9. Changes
We may update this Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Bot after changes means you accept the updated Policy.
